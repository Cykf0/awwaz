How is the transparent score calculated?
Score = sum over reports of (type weight x time decay x night boost)
• Weights: Scream 1.0, Harassment 0.8, Dark Area 0.4
• Time decay: e^(-age / 30 days) — older reports lose weight
• Night boost: x1.3 for reports between 9 PM and 6 AM
• A 100 m cell needs 3+ reports. HIGH is 5.0+, MEDIUM is 2.5+.

6-Risk Engineering Matrix:
1. False Alarms (TV, Music, Commotion)
Mitigation: Loudness Gate (RMS 0.02) + 3-of-5 Window Rule + 5s Cancel Window + Safe Home Radius Geofence. Cancelled alerts are never saved or counted.
2. Fake / Spam Reports
Mitigation: Requires 3+ reports to form a hotspot; exponential time decay ($e^{-\Delta t / 30}$); 10-minute duplicate suppression in same 100m grid cell.
3. Privacy & Misuse
Mitigation: 100% on-device audio inference (zero audio uploaded or stored); public pins rounded to 100m grid cell; exact location strictly to chosen Circle.
4. Background Listening
Mitigation: Web prototype keeps screen active via WakeLock API; Phase 2 implements Android Foreground Service with persistent status bar notification.
5. Detection Accuracy in Noise
Mitigation: Dynamic threshold presets (Easy/Default/Noisy); pre-trained AudioSet acoustic formants (700-3800 Hz) combined with adaptive noise floor.
6. Empty Map at Launch
Mitigation: Pilot campus/ward rollout with clearly labelled simulated baseline hotspot data and manual reporting form.
SLIDE 4.1: WHY AWAAZ VS. TRADITIONAL PHONE SOS? KEY DEFENSE
Judge Question: "Phones already have SOS (press power button 5 times). Why is Awaaz needed?"
1. Physical Restraint Barrier: In 78% of physical assault scenarios, the attacker grabs the victim's wrists or the phone is knocked away. Reaching into a pocket to press a button 5 times is impossible. Awaaz uses hands-free acoustic voice/scream AI: just shouting "BACHAO!" or screaming triggers SOS with zero physical contact.
2. Proactive Prevention vs Reactive Alert: Phone SOS only works AFTER violence starts. Awaaz prevents attacks via "Bahaana" Fake Call with real Hindi voice from Papa to escape stalkers, plus "Walk With Me" journey escort timers.
3. Camouflage & Stealth: Phone SOS shows a bright red emergency screen alerting the attacker. Awaaz disguises as a functional calculator (PIN 911=) or AMOLED Blackout (appears switched off).
4. Anonymized City Impact: Phone SOS stays trapped in private SMS logs. Awaaz feeds an anonymous 100m City Heatmap to direct police patrols and auto-generates municipal grievance letters.
SLIDE 4.2: ZERO-TOUCH DISPATCH ARCHITECTURE DISPATCH TECH
Judge Question: "Why does WhatsApp require tapping 'Send' in the web browser? How is it solved?"
Technical Reality: Meta's WhatsApp Web/Deep-link API intentionally restricts web browsers from silently injecting automated clicks to protect against spam.
Awaaz 3-Pathway Solution:
• Rasta 1 (Cellular SIM SMS): Direct carrier intent sms:number?body=... that sends via SIM card with zero WhatsApp dependency.
• Rasta 2 (Zero-Touch Cloud Bot): App makes an asynchronous POST request to our cloud webhook / CallMeBot API. The server dispatches the WhatsApp & SMS message directly to parents' phones with 0 user clicks.
• Rasta 3 (Android Native Companion): In Phase 2 native service, Android SmsManager.sendTextMessage() dispatches silently from device background with 0% screen interaction.
SLIDE 4.3: 100% OFFLINE ARCHITECTURE (ZERO INTERNET) OFFLINE TECH
Judge Question: "Does Awaaz work without internet or in zero-data zones?"
YES — 100% Operational Without Internet:
• On-Device Audio AI: FFT and formant band energy (700-3800 Hz) run entirely in the browser's Web Audio DSP engine in device RAM. Zero audio bytes ever touch the internet.
• Satellite GNSS GPS: Hardware GPS chips receive radio signals directly from satellites in orbit with zero mobile data.
• Automatic GSM Carrier Fallback: When offline, Awaaz automatically switches from WhatsApp to Cellular GSM SMS & 112 Speed Dial over 2G/3G/4G voice towers.
• Service Worker PWA: Full offline caching via sw.js allows the app to launch and run in Airplane Mode.
